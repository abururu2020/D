In the darkness of the sea, inside a great fish, one prophet called out to Allah. And Allah answered him. This is the story of Yunus, peace be upon him, told the way the Quran tells it.

Allah says: And indeed, Yunus was among the messengers. He was sent to his people, to call them to worship Allah alone.

The Prophet Muhammad, peace and blessings be upon him, said: No servant should say that I am better than Yunus bin Matta. That is in Sahih al-Bukhari.

Yunus called his people to Allah. Then, the Quran says, he went off in anger.

He went to a ship, fully loaded, and set out on the sea.

On the ship, lots were drawn. And Yunus was among the losers.

Then the fish swallowed him. Allah calls him the man of the fish, and the companion of the fish.

Inside the fish, within the darknesses, Yunus called out.

He said: There is no god except You. Glory be to You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers.

Allah says: So We answered him, and saved him from the distress. And this is how We save the believers.

And had he not been of those who glorify Allah, he would have stayed inside its belly until the Day people are raised again.

The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said that no Muslim calls on Allah with this prayer, for anything, except that Allah answers him. That is in the collection of at-Tirmidhi, and scholars graded it authentic.

Then the fish cast him onto the open shore, and he was ill.

And Allah made a gourd vine grow over him, to give him shade.

And his Lord chose him, and made him one of the righteous.

Then Allah sent him to a hundred thousand people, or more.

And they believed. So Allah let them enjoy their lives for a time.

The Quran says there was no town whose faith benefited it, except the people of Yunus. When they believed, Allah removed from them the punishment of disgrace in this life.

Darkness all around him. And still, his prayer was heard.

Whatever darkness you are in, call on Allah as Yunus did. There is no god except You. Glory be to You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers.

That is the story of Yunus.
