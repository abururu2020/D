Nine hundred and fifty years. That is how long one prophet lived among his people, calling them to Allah. This is the story of Nuh, peace be upon him, told the way the Quran tells it.

On the Day of Judgment, people will come to Nuh and say: You are the first of the messengers to the people of the earth. That is in a hadith of the Prophet Muhammad, peace and blessings be upon him, in Sahih al-Bukhari.

Among the people of Nuh there were righteous men. When those men died, Satan whispered to the people: Set up statues where they used to sit, and call them by their names.

Ibn Abbas, may Allah be pleased with him, explained it, as Sahih al-Bukhari records. At first, no one worshipped the statues. But the generations passed, the knowledge was forgotten, and then the statues were worshipped.

Their names were Wadd, Suwa, Yaghuth, Yauq and Nasr. And Allah sent Nuh to his people with one message.

He said: O my people, worship Allah. You have no god other than Him. I fear for you the punishment of a tremendous day.

The leaders of his people said: We see you in clear error. Nuh said: There is no error in me. I am a messenger from the Lord of the worlds.

He called them night and day. But every call only made them run further away.

Every time he invited them to be forgiven, they put their fingers in their ears. They covered themselves with their garments. They insisted, and they grew more arrogant.

So he called them openly, and he spoke to them in private.

He said: Ask forgiveness of your Lord. He is ever forgiving. He will send rain from the sky upon you in showers, and give you wealth and children, and gardens and rivers.

He said: Do you not see how Allah created seven heavens in layers? He made the moon a light in them, and the sun a burning lamp.

But the leaders only mocked. They said: You are only a man like us. And the only ones who follow you are the lowest of us.

Nuh said: I ask you for no payment. My reward is only from Allah. And I will not drive away the believers.

They said: O Nuh, you have argued with us, and argued too much. So bring us what you threaten us with, if you are truthful.

Then they said: If you do not stop, O Nuh, you will surely be stoned. And they called him a madman.

Year after year, only a few believed. Then Allah revealed to Nuh: No one else from your people will believe, except those who already have. So do not grieve over what they do.

And Allah commanded him: Build the ship under Our watch and Our inspiration.

So Nuh built the ship, of planks and nails. And every time the leaders of his people passed by, they laughed at him.

He said: If you laugh at us, we will laugh at you, just as you laugh. And you will come to know who will receive a punishment that disgraces him.

Then the command of Allah came. And the oven overflowed.

Allah said: Load onto the ship two of every kind, a male and a female, and your family, except those against whom the word has already gone out. And take those who believe. And only a few believed with him.

Nuh said: Board it. In the name of Allah is its sailing and its anchoring. My Lord is forgiving and merciful.

Then the gates of the sky opened, with water pouring down. And the earth burst with springs. And the waters met, for a matter already decreed.

The ship sailed with them through waves like mountains. It sailed under the watch of Allah.

And Nuh called to his son, who was standing apart: O my son, come aboard with us, and do not be with the disbelievers.

His son said: I will go to a mountain. It will protect me from the water.

Nuh said: Today there is no protector from the decree of Allah, except for the one He has mercy on.

And a wave came between them. And he was among the drowned.

Then it was said: O earth, swallow your water. O sky, hold back your rain. And the water sank away, and the matter was done.

The ship came to rest on al-Judi. And it was said: Away with the wrongdoing people.

Nuh called to his Lord. He said: My Lord, my son is of my family. And Your promise is true, and You are the most just of judges.

Allah said: O Nuh, he is not of your family. His deeds were not righteous. So do not ask Me about what you have no knowledge of.

Nuh said: My Lord, I seek refuge in You from asking what I have no knowledge of. Unless You forgive me and have mercy on me, I will be among the losers.

And it was said: O Nuh, come down in peace from Us, with blessings on you, and on the nations from those who are with you.

Allah saved him and the people of the ship, and made it a sign for all the worlds.

And Allah left for him a good mention among later generations: Peace upon Nuh among the worlds. He was one of Allah's believing servants.

And on the Day of Judgment, Nuh will be asked: Did you deliver the message? He will say: Yes, my Lord. His people will say: No prophet came to us.

Then Nuh will be asked: Who will testify for you? He will say: Muhammad, peace and blessings be upon him, and his nation. And they will testify that he delivered the message. That is in Sahih al-Bukhari.

Nine hundred and fifty years of patience. The ship stands as a sign for anyone who will remember. That is the story of Nuh.
