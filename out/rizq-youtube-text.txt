DESCRIPTION
Have you ever wondered why your rizq feels so limited, even when you work hard and make dua? Rizq is not just money. It is your health, your family, your peace of mind, your time, and the faith in your heart.

In this video we go through eight ways the Quran and authentic Sunnah teach us to seek Allah's provision and barakah: istighfar, taqwa, tawakkul, family ties, charity, gratitude, halal income and dua.
