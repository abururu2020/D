TITLE
The Story of Prophet Yunus (Jonah) | A Prayer from the Darkness | Quran Stories

DESCRIPTION
The story of Prophet Yunus, peace be upon him, told the way the Quran tells it. He boarded a laden ship, lots were drawn, and a great fish swallowed him. Within the darknesses he called out: "There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers" (21:87). And Allah answered him.

Then he was cast onto the shore, a gourd vine grew over him, and he was sent to a hundred thousand people or more, and they believed. Every verse quoted is shown on screen with its reference.
