TITLE
The Story of Prophet Nuh (Noah) | 950 Years, the Ark and the Flood | Quran Stories

DESCRIPTION
The story of Prophet Nuh, peace be upon him, told the way the Quran tells it. He remained among his people for a thousand years less fifty (29:14), calling them to worship Allah alone, night and day, in public and in private.

They mocked him, called him a madman and threatened to stone him. Then Allah commanded him to build the ship. The oven overflowed, the gates of the sky opened, and the ship sailed through waves like mountains. Every verse quoted is shown on screen with its reference.
