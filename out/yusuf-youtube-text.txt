TITLE
The Story of Prophet Yusuf (Joseph) | From the Well to the Throne | Quran Stories

DESCRIPTION
The story of Prophet Yusuf, peace be upon him, told the way the Quran tells it. Allah calls it "the best of stories" (12:3), and it is told from beginning to end in a single surah: Surah Yusuf.

A boy's dream of eleven stars, the sun and the moon. Brothers who threw him into a well. A false accusation, years in prison, the king's dream, and a reunion that ends with forgiveness. Every verse quoted here is from Surah Yusuf, with its reference on screen.
