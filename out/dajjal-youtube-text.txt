TITLE
Who Is Dajjal and Why Will He Be So Dangerous?

DESCRIPTION
A man will appear who travels the earth faster than the wind, brings rain to those who follow him and drought to those who refuse, and comes with what looks like Paradise and Hellfire, but his Paradise is Hellfire and his Hellfire is Paradise. Every prophet, even Prophet Nuh (peace be upon him), warned his people about him.

Who is Al-Masih Ad-Dajjal, how will believers recognize him, what are his forty days, which two cities he can never enter, how his story ends, and the protection the Prophet ﷺ taught us, all from authentic hadith.
