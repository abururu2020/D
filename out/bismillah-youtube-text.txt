DESCRIPTION
Muslims say it thousands of times: before eating, before drinking, before starting something important. But what actually happens when you say Bismillah, and what happens when you don't?

The Prophet ﷺ told us that when a person mentions Allah's name on entering the home and before eating, Shaytan finds no place to spend the night and no evening meal. In this video: what Bismillah really means, the manners of eating he ﷺ taught a young boy, what to say if you forget, saying Bismillah when you enter your home, and how to keep these words from becoming just a habit.
